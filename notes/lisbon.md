# Lisbon Travel Knowledge Base

This document is designed for retrieval-augmented answers in a travel assistant.
It focuses on practical guidance for short trips, especially in October.

## Best Neighborhoods (First-Time Visitors)

### Baixa / Chiado
- Best for: first-time visitors, walkability, shopping, central sightseeing.
- Pros: very central, easy transit links, many cafes/restaurants.
- Tradeoffs: can feel touristy and pricier than outer areas.

### Alfama
- Best for: historic atmosphere, viewpoints, traditional streets, Fado vibe.
- Pros: charming old Lisbon character, iconic photo spots.
- Tradeoffs: hilly terrain, cobblestones, fewer direct ride pickups in narrow streets.

### Bairro Alto / Cais do Sodre
- Best for: nightlife, bars, social evenings.
- Pros: strong evening scene, many dining options.
- Tradeoffs: can be noisy at night; not ideal for light sleepers.

### Principe Real
- Best for: quieter upscale stay, design shops, cafes.
- Pros: safer-feeling, relaxed atmosphere, good food scene.
- Tradeoffs: accommodation prices can be higher.

### Parque das Nacoes
- Best for: modern area, family-friendly walks, less chaotic environment.
- Pros: wide streets, waterfront promenades, modern buildings.
- Tradeoffs: less historic Lisbon charm; farther from old-town landmarks.

## Safety Notes

- Lisbon is generally considered safe for tourists.
- Main risk is petty theft (pickpocketing), especially:
  - Tram 28
  - crowded viewpoints
  - busy central squares and transit hubs
- Practical precautions:
  - keep phone/wallet in front pocket or zipped bag
  - avoid placing phone on outdoor cafe tables
  - use cross-body bags in crowded places
- Late night:
  - use rideshare/taxi for steep or less busy routes
  - stay in well-lit streets

## Transport Guide

- Metro is efficient for airport and major routes.
- Trams and buses are useful for hills and old neighborhoods.
- Walking is great in center, but expect steep streets and stairs.
- Popular options:
  - Viva Viagem card for public transport
  - rideshare for night returns or hill-heavy legs
- Airport access:
  - Metro is budget-friendly
  - taxi/rideshare faster with luggage

## October Travel Conditions

- Typical daytime: mild to warm.
- Typical evening: cooler; light jacket recommended.
- Rain: possible occasional showers.
- What to pack:
  - light layers
  - compact rain shell or umbrella
  - comfortable shoes with good grip (cobblestones can be slippery)

## Accommodation Decision Heuristics

Use these quick rules when comparing listings:

- If user prioritizes nightlife:
  - prefer Bairro Alto / Cais do Sodre
  - warn about noise and suggest top-floor/quiet-street units

- If user prioritizes quiet + central:
  - prefer Principe Real or edges of Chiado
  - avoid streets with dense bar clusters

- If user prioritizes historical vibe:
  - prefer Alfama
  - warn about steep climbs and accessibility

- If user has kids/stroller/heavy luggage:
  - prefer Baixa or Parque das Nacoes
  - filter for elevator and step-free entry

## Budget Bands (Heuristic, Not Real-Time Pricing)

- Budget: smaller studios/rooms, farther from prime center or with fewer amenities.
- Mid-range: central apartments with standard amenities.
- Higher-end: premium location, views, larger spaces, better design/amenities.

Always confirm final pricing in the booking platform due to seasonality and demand.

## Common Clarifying Questions Before Booking

Ask user for:
- exact check-in/check-out dates
- number of adults/children
- budget range per night
- noise tolerance (low/medium/high)
- must-have amenities (elevator, AC, kitchen, washer, wifi quality)
- trip style (nightlife, culture, family, remote work)

## Response Format Suggestion

When answering Lisbon planning queries, structure as:
1. Recommended neighborhoods with tradeoffs
2. Suggested stays or search strategy
3. Practical travel tips
4. Missing info needed to finalize booking shortlist
